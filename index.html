About Me

Hi! I'm Cheng Wang, a Research Assistant at UC San Diego, advised by Prof. Tzu-Mao Li. I have developed a strong passion for Visual Computing, particularly interested in inverse problems. I recently earned my Master's degree in Computer Science from UC San Diego in June 2024. Before that, I received my Bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Technology at Chu Kochen College of Zhejiang University, advised by Prof. Rui Wang.
